# Illustrative Data and Assumptions

## Data classification
All entities, initiative names, scores, cost estimates, dates, benefit targets, governance decisions, and scenarios in this folder are fictional and illustrative. Values are in PKR millions unless noted otherwise.

## Financial assumptions
- Estimates are planning-level approximations, not vendor quotes.
- FY1 table assumes full-year recurring costs for comparability; actual phasing depends on contract start and acceptance.
- Worked PAM case uses PKR 42m implementation plus PKR 13m annual recurring cost; three-year simple TCO = PKR 81m.
- The portfolio budget illustration totals PKR 259m change and PKR 79m FY1 run, for PKR 338m.
- Taxes, inflation, foreign exchange, discount rate, capitalization, and financing costs are excluded unless expressly stated.
- Benefits are not guaranteed and must be validated against a baseline.

## Method assumptions
- Risk scores use 1–5 scales and the stated weights.
- Scoring supports structured judgment; evidence and accountable risk ownership remain essential.
- Mandatory obligations override weighted rank.
- Example targets are to be calibrated following discovery and baseline measurement.

## Limitations
This package is a reference portfolio artifact, not implementation advice for a specific organization. Adapt it to actual risk appetite, regulatory obligations, procurement rules, architecture, finance policy, and operating model.
