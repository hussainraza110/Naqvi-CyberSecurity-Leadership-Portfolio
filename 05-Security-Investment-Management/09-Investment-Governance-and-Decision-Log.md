# Investment Governance and Decision Log

## Decision rights
| Forum / role | Decision |
|---|---|
| CISO | Portfolio recommendation, security design guardrails, prioritization |
| Business sponsor | Business value, adoption, service impact |
| Risk owner | Residual risk acceptance within delegated authority |
| Finance | Budget validation, forecast, financial assumptions |
| Architecture review | Design fit, integration, lifecycle, technical debt |
| Investment committee | Funding, sequencing, trade-offs within mandate |
| Board / Risk Committee | Material risk appetite, strategic investment, or delegated escalation |

## Decision workflow
1. Submit complete intake and risk statement.
2. Challenge options, cost, confidence, dependencies, and benefits.
3. Confirm mandatory status and delegated authority.
4. Record decision, conditions, owner, funding, and review date.
5. Track conditions to closure; escalate exceptions and missed outcomes.

## Decision log template
| ID | Date | Proposal | Decision | Funding | Conditions | Decision owner | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| DEC-001 | YYYY-MM-DD | Example proposal | Approve / defer / reject | PKR m | Defined conditions | Role | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Exception / risk acceptance
Record risk scenario, affected service, compensating controls, risk owner, approval authority, expiry, monitoring, and remediation plan. Expired acceptances are escalated; they are not silently renewed.

## Governance cadence
Monthly portfolio delivery and financial review; quarterly investment committee; annual budget and strategic alignment review; ad hoc escalation for material incident, regulatory deadline, cost overrun, or risk appetite breach.
