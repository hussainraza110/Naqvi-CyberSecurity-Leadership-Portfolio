# Cybersecurity Budget and Forecast

## Budget model
Maintain separate views for:
- **Run:** recurring licenses, managed services, staffing, support, and operations.
- **Change:** implementation, migration, consulting, and project-specific costs.
- **Contingency:** explicitly governed reserve tied to estimate uncertainty.
- **Capital / operating classification:** determined by Finance under applicable accounting policy.

## Forecast controls
- Monthly actual vs budget vs forecast (EAC).
- Explain variance by volume, price, scope, timing, FX, or delivery slippage.
- Forecast formula: **Estimate at Completion (EAC) = actual cost to date + forecast remaining cost**.
- Track committed, invoiced, and paid amounts separately.
- Identify renewal dates and price escalators at least 120 days in advance.
- Finance validates allocation, capitalization, tax, FX, and accrual treatment.

## Scenarios
| Scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Base | Approved scope and current estimate |
| Downside | FX increase, delayed procurement, additional integration |
| Upside | Consolidation, shared services, or negotiated discounts |

Do not treat risk reduction as cashable savings unless a budget line or expense is demonstrably reduced.
