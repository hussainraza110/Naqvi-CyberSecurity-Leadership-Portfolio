## Portfolio composition
The portfolio balances preventive controls, detection, resilience, data protection, secure delivery, supplier risk, exposure management, and human capability. Dependencies include identity foundations for privileged access, telemetry onboarding for SIEM, service ownership and recovery testing for resilience, and asset/data inventories for risk prioritization.

## Portfolio controls
- Each initiative has one accountable sponsor and one delivery owner.
- Costs are shown as estimate ranges at early stage and refined at approval.
- Funding is staged at discovery, design, build, and operational acceptance gates.
- Reprioritization is permitted when risk evidence or delivery capacity changes.
- Portfolio status must include risk outcome, delivery confidence, spend, and decision required—not just schedule.
