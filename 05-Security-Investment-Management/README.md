## Operating principles
1. Fund risk reduction and resilience outcomes—not tool acquisition in isolation.
2. Compare investments using a consistent scoring model and documented assumptions.
3. Separate mandatory control obligations from discretionary optimization.
4. Track total cost of ownership, delivery dependencies, residual risk, and realized benefits.
5. Reassess when threat conditions, business strategy, architecture, or estimates change.
