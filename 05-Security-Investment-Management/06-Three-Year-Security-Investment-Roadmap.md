# Three-Year Security Investment Roadmap

> Illustrative sequencing for Meridian Financial Group; dates and capacity are assumptions.

## Roadmap logic
Prioritize foundational identity, asset, service, and telemetry visibility; then scale control coverage and operationalize measurable outcomes. The roadmap is dependency-aware, not a commitment to fixed dates before discovery.

| Workstream | Year 1 — establish | Year 2 — scale | Year 3 — optimize |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity / PAM | Tier-0 pilot, inventory, vault and workflow | Expand to critical platforms and applications | Adaptive access, mature recertification |
| Detection | Log-source baseline, use-case tuning | Expand coverage and automation | Detection quality analytics and continuous tuning |
| Resilience | Critical service mapping and recovery tests | Remediate recovery gaps; exercise scenarios | Improve recovery automation and evidence |
| Data protection | Discovery pilot and classification patterns | Extend to high-risk repositories | Policy optimization and lifecycle controls |
| Secure delivery | CI/CD baseline and pilot teams | Embed controls across product teams | Measure defect trends and developer experience |
| Third-party risk | Segment critical suppliers | Integrate assurance and remediation workflow | Concentration analysis and continuous signals |
| Exposure management | Asset normalization and risk-based SLAs | Integrate threat intelligence and ownership | Exposure reduction optimization |
| People | Role-based learning baseline | Targeted campaigns and simulations | Measure behavioral and incident outcomes |

## Stage gates
- **G0 — Intake:** risk owner, problem statement, initial estimate.
- **G1 — Discovery:** validated scope, options, dependencies, estimate range.
- **G2 — Funding:** approved business case and budget.
- **G3 — Design:** architecture, control requirements, operational model.
- **G4 — Pilot:** acceptance criteria and pilot evidence.
- **G5 — Production:** operational acceptance, support, monitoring, rollback.
- **G6 — Benefits:** baseline-to-target review and residual-risk decision.

## Dependencies
Asset and service ownership underpin exposure management; identity foundations support PAM; log source quality supports detection; service recovery objectives and business impact analysis underpin resilience; product ownership and pipeline standards support secure delivery.
