# Framework and Regulatory Alignment

## Mapping approach
This mapping is illustrative and intended to organize investment evidence. It is not a control certification, legal opinion, or assertion that the fictional enterprise meets any requirement. Confirm current versions and jurisdiction-specific obligations with accountable compliance and legal teams.

| Investment management activity | NIST CSF 2.0 relationship | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy, roles, decision rights | GV.OC, GV.RR, GV.PO | Clauses 4–6; Annex A organizational controls |
| Risk assessment and prioritization | GV.RM, ID.RA | Clause 6.1; risk assessment and treatment |
| Portfolio planning and funding | GV.RM, GV.OV | Clause 6; Clause 7 support |
| Supplier investment decisions | GV.SC | Annex A supplier relationship controls |
| Delivery and operational implementation | PR, DE, RS, RC functions as relevant | Annex A controls; Clauses 8–10 |
| Measurement and reporting | GV.OV, ID.IM | Clauses 9–10 |
| Continual improvement | ID.IM | Clause 10 |

## Evidence examples
- Approved risk methodology and risk appetite alignment.
- Investment intake, business cases, and recorded decisions.
- Budget and forecast evidence.
- Control implementation and operational acceptance records.
- Metric definitions, baseline, and benefits evidence.
- Risk acceptance with authority, expiry, and compensating controls.
- Management review and improvement actions.

## Regulatory mapping caveat
Specific financial-sector requirements vary by jurisdiction, regulator, service, and effective date. Map each obligation to the actual source text, applicability decision, accountable owner, due date, and evidence artifact. Do not infer compliance from framework alignment alone.
