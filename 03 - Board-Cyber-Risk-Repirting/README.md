# 03 — Board Cyber Risk Reporting

Portfolio package for board-level cyber risk oversight at **Meridian Financial Group**, a fictional large regulated financial-services enterprise spanning banking, digital payments, and investment services.

> All enterprise details, metrics, thresholds, scenarios, events, and financial estimates are fictional and illustrative. No confidential employer information is included.

## Contents
- `01-Playbook/` — governance, reporting cadence, workflow, escalation
- `02-Board-Artifacts/` — quarterly board report and dashboard
- `03-Metrics-and-Appetite/` — appetite, tolerance, KPI/KRI framework
- `04-Worked-Example/` — fictional reporting cycle and board challenge
- `05-Templates/` — reusable reporting templates
- `06-Diagrams/` — Mermaid workflow diagrams
- `07-Reference/` — alignment notes and assumptions

The board report is a decision instrument, not a technical activity inventory. It should explain what changed, business impact, appetite position, management response, and the decision or challenge required.
