# Cyber Risk Appetite & Tolerance — Meridian Financial Group

> All values below are fictional scenario assumptions, not regulatory requirements or real Meridian data.

## Definitions
- **Appetite:** risk Meridian is willing to accept while pursuing objectives.
- **Tolerance:** operational boundary; crossing it requires defined action.
- **Capacity:** maximum exposure sustainable before objectives or obligations become untenable.
- **Exception:** documented, time-bound authorization outside a threshold by delegated authority.

## Illustrative threshold register
| Measure | Within appetite | Near limit | Above appetite / tolerance | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Privileged accounts with phishing-resistant MFA | ≥98% | 95–<98% | <95% | CIO |
| Critical internet-facing vulnerabilities remediated within 7 days | ≥95% | 90–<95% | <90% | Technology owners |
| Critical-service recovery tests meeting objective | 100% | 90–<100% | <90% | COO / CIO |
| High-severity alert triage within SLA | ≥98% | 95–<98% | <95% | CISO |
| Material suppliers with current assurance evidence | ≥95% | 90–<95% | <90% | Procurement / service owners |
| Critical cyber-risk actions overdue >30 days | 0 | 1–2 | ≥3 | Risk owners |

## Escalation
1. Metric owner validates breach and data quality.
2. CISO and CRO assess materiality, duration, concentration, and business consequence.
3. Risk owner provides treatment, owner, date, interim controls, and residual exposure.
4. Material service/customer/financial/regulatory implications follow incident and governance escalation protocols.
5. Exceptions specify rationale, compensating controls, expiry, and approval authority.
6. A threshold breach is not automatically a board risk acceptance.

## Reporting conventions
Show actual, threshold, prior trend, and owner. Include denominator and cut-off. Distinguish appetite breach from control defect or incomplete evidence. Identify leading, lagging, or contextual measures.
