# Framework Alignment and Scenario Assumptions

## Alignment approach
This artifact uses broadly recognized governance concepts; it does not certify compliance or claim a framework mandates these exact thresholds.
- **NIST CSF 2.0:** Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover; connect cyber reporting to enterprise oversight.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** ISMS governance, risk treatment, performance evaluation, continual improvement.
- **ISO 22301:** continuity and resilience governance, exercising, evaluation.
- **COBIT:** governance objectives, performance monitoring, management accountability.
- **Three Lines Model:** distinguish management ownership, risk oversight, and independent assurance.

Confirm mappings against current source materials and the organization's control framework before operational use.

## Assumptions
- Meridian Financial Group is fictional: large regulated financial services, banking, digital payments, investment services.
- Hybrid technology estate; quarterly board reporting and event-driven escalation.
- Metrics, thresholds, incidents, service counts, maturity claims, and due dates are invented.
- This package does not claim Meridian complies with any law, regulation, or framework.
- Business-impact estimates are scenario narratives unless independently quantified.
- No confidential employer information is included.

## Limitations
Metric reliability depends on source inventories and definitions. Dashboard status is not a substitute for analysis. A technical recovery test alone does not prove business continuity. Supplier evidence can be stale or incomplete. Disclose uncertainty and avoid unsupported precision.
