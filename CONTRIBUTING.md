# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving this cybersecurity leadership portfolio.

## Scope

Contributions should strengthen one or more portfolio areas:

- CISO strategy and operating model
- Board cyber-risk reporting
- Cyber crisis management and resilience
- Security investment and business value
- Organization and talent leadership
- Executive case studies

## Contribution Standards

Please ensure proposed material:

1. Is clear, practical, and suitable for a security leadership or executive audience.
2. Connects cybersecurity activity to business outcomes, risk, governance, or resilience.
3. Identifies assumptions and distinguishes facts from illustrative scenarios.
4. Uses consistent terminology and explains specialized terms where needed.
5. Includes meaningful ownership, decision points, measures, or implementation guidance where applicable.
6. Is original or used with appropriate permission and attribution.

## Confidentiality and Safety

Do not submit:

- Confidential, proprietary, classified, or employer-restricted information
- Real credentials, secrets, tokens, private keys, or sensitive system details
- Unnecessary personal data
- Third-party copyrighted material without permission or a valid basis for use

Use fictional, synthetic, or appropriately anonymized examples. Anonymization must not leave details that could reasonably identify an organization, individual, system, or incident.

## Pull Requests

For a proposed change:

1. Explain the objective and intended audience.
2. Identify the portfolio area affected.
3. Describe substantive changes and assumptions.
4. Check links, formatting, terminology, and references.
5. Confirm that the contribution does not disclose restricted information.

## Review

Contributions may be reviewed for accuracy, clarity, relevance, confidentiality, and consistency with the repository's purpose. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.
