# Cybersecurity Leadership Portfolio

An executive-focused cybersecurity leadership portfolio covering security strategy, enterprise risk management, governance, cyber resilience, security investment, and organizational leadership.

## Purpose

This repository demonstrates practical approaches to aligning cybersecurity with business objectives, enterprise risk, and executive decision-making. It is designed to communicate how a security leader frames problems, establishes direction, measures outcomes, and enables resilient operations.

The materials are professional portfolio artifacts—not representations of any employer's confidential policies, systems, or internal decisions.

## Leadership Philosophy

- **Business alignment:** Security priorities should support business objectives and be expressed in business terms.
- **Risk-based decisions:** Focus on material risk, credible threat scenarios, and risk reduction.
- **Accountability:** Define ownership, decision rights, escalation paths, and risk acceptance authority.
- **Resilience:** Prepare to sustain and restore critical services, not merely prevent incidents.
- **Evidence and outcomes:** Use meaningful measures to assess effectiveness and support decisions.
- **Pragmatism:** Apply controls proportionately to business context, exposure, and resources.

## Portfolio Areas

1. **CISO Strategy & Operating Model** — strategy, target state, governance, operating model, and roadmaps.
2. **Board Risk Reporting** — executive cyber-risk narratives, indicators, appetite, and decision briefs.
3. **Cyber Crisis & Resilience** — incident leadership, crisis governance, continuity, and recovery.
4. **Security Investment & Business Value** — prioritization, business cases, options analysis, and value measurement.
5. **Organization & Talent Leadership** — capability models, roles, workforce planning, and leadership development.
6. **Executive Case Studies** — anonymized or fictional scenarios showing decisions, trade-offs, and outcomes.
