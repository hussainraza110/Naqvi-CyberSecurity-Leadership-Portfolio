# Security Leadership Scorecard

| Dimension | Measure | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Risk | Risks outside appetite; overdue actions | Exposure and accountability |
| Service | SLA and stakeholder satisfaction | Responsiveness |
| Control | Critical control coverage and test | Effectiveness |
| Delivery | Milestones and outcome acceptance | Confidence |
| Financial | Actual / forecast vs budget | Stewardship |
| People | Skill coverage, attrition, workload | Capability and sustainability |
| Resilience | Deputy and runbook test coverage | Continuity |

Set thresholds from approved appetite and baselines. Every red indicator needs impact, owner, action, and date. Never average away a critical risk. Narrative: what changed, business impact, decision required, management action.
