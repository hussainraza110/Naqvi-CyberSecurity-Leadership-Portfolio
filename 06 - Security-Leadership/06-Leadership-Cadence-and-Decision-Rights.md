# Leadership Cadence and Decision Rights

| Forum | Frequency | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Security leadership team | Weekly | Actions, dependencies, escalation |
| Risk/control review | Monthly | Risk decisions and owners |
| Portfolio/finance review | Monthly | Forecast and reprioritization |
| Talent/capability review | Quarterly | Workforce actions |
| Executive cyber-risk review | Quarterly | Decisions and challenge |
| Strategy refresh | Annual / event-driven | Strategy and operating model update |

Decision record: decision, options, evidence, accountable maker, rationale, accepted risk, action owner, due date, review trigger. Escalate appetite breaches, threatened mandatory dates, material outages, forecast tolerance breaches, uncovered critical roles, or blocked decisions.
