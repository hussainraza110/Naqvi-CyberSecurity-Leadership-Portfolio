# Performance Management and Objectives

Objectives connect to business outcomes and should be measurable and within the employee's influence.

| Objective | Measure | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Improve detection quality | Validated coverage and false-positive trend | Monthly |
| Improve response readiness | Exercise actions closed and playbook quality | Quarterly |
| Strengthen access governance | Timely reviews and exception aging | Monthly |
| Improve delivery partnership | Stakeholder feedback and secure design adoption | Quarterly |
| Build team resilience | Cross-training, runbooks, deputy coverage | Quarterly |

Set expectations before the period, assess evidence and context, calibrate across teams, document feedback, and agree development actions. Do not reward alert volume or closure counts without risk context, nor normalize chronic overtime.
