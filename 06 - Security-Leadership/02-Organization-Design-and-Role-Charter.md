# Organization Design and Role Charter

## Illustrative structure
CISO-led domains: Governance/Risk/Compliance; Architecture/Engineering; Security Operations/Incident Response; IAM; Product Security; Resilience; Third-Party Security. This is a reference structure, not a mandatory hierarchy.

## Role charter template
- Role title and mission
- Accountable outcomes and decision rights
- Core responsibilities and required capabilities
- Stakeholders and escalation conditions
- Measures and deputy / delegation
- Review date

## Decision accountability
| Decision | Accountable | Consulted |
|---|---|---|
| Security strategy | CISO | CIO, CRO, business leaders |
| Risk acceptance | Designated risk owner | CISO, Risk |
| Security standard | Security architecture lead | Enterprise Architecture |
| Incident command | Incident commander under plan | CISO, BCM |
| Workforce plan | CISO / functional leader | HR, Finance |
| Product remediation | Product / service owner | Security partner |

Span of control depends on complexity, risk, workload, geography, and maturity—not a universal ratio.
