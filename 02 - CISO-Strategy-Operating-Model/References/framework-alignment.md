# Framework Alignment and Reference Notes

This portfolio uses frameworks as reference points; it does not claim compliance, certification, or complete mapping.

- **NIST CSF 2.0:** high-level outcomes and governance. https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
- **ISO/IEC 27001:** ISMS context, leadership, risk treatment, performance, continual improvement. Verify edition and scope. https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
- **COBIT:** enterprise governance and management of information and technology. https://www.isaca.org/resources/cobit
- **NIST SP 800-37:** risk-management lifecycle context. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final
- **NIST SP 800-53:** control reference where relevant; detailed control mapping is outside this strategy package. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final

Verify current editions and jurisdictional applicability. Framework alignment is not proof of regulatory compliance. Distinguish binding requirements from recommendations and illustrative assumptions.
