# CISO Lifecycle Playbook

## A. Greenfield establishment
**Days 1–30:** confirm mandate/sponsor; understand business, services, appetite, obligations; review incidents, audit themes, exposure, people, contracts, tools; establish escalation and incident leadership.  
**Days 31–90:** define initial strategy, risk priorities, governance, minimum viable capabilities, 12-month roadmap, investment case, owners, and baseline metrics.  
**Months 4–12:** implement foundations, integrate security into critical change/sourcing, exercise response/recovery, validate workforce/provider model, conduct annual review.

## B. Transformation
**Diagnose:** validate strategy, capabilities, operating model, performance, critical service exposure, incidents, exceptions, supplier dependencies, skills, cost, and capacity.  
**Design:** agree outcomes and risk-based target; clarify decision rights and service delivery; prioritize portfolio and change plan.  
**Execute:** stabilize urgent risk/governance; deliver incrementally; track risk movement, benefits, adoption, and constraints; adjust based on evidence.

## C. Strategic refresh
Reassess business strategy, services, threat scenarios, appetite, incidents, exercises, assurance, technology and sourcing changes. Challenge investment and capability relevance; identify simplification/emerging needs; refresh strategy, funding, measures, and commitments.

## Decision gates
| Gate | Question | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Understand business and critical services? | Strategy, inventory, stakeholder inputs |
| Risk | Material scenarios and owners known? | Profiles, appetite, incidents, dependencies |
| Design | Operating model feasible/accountable? | Decision rights, capabilities, capacity |
| Investment | Options and costs justified? | Business cases, risk reduction, dependencies |
| Execution | Outcomes and risk tracked? | Milestones, metrics, issues, benefits |
| Refresh | Context/evidence changed? | Strategy review, performance, business change |

## Failure modes
Strategy without business ownership; target maturity without rationale; roadmap beyond capacity; CISO claiming business risk ownership; activity-only metrics; investment without benefits owner; duplicate forums; exceptions that never expire.
