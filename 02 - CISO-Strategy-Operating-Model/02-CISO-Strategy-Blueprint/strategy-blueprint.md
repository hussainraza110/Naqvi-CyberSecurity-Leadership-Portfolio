# Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy Blueprint

## Strategy statement
Meridian will protect customer trust and enable secure business growth by managing cyber risk as enterprise risk, strengthening critical-service resilience, embedding security in business and technology decisions, and measuring executive-relevant outcomes.

## Vision
A trusted, resilient, and secure financial-services enterprise where cybersecurity enables sustainable digital business.

## Mission
Reduce material cyber risk and support business objectives through risk-informed governance, resilient services, effective capabilities, and accountable execution.

## Principles
Business-led; risk-informed; service-centric; accountable; secure-by-design; resilience-oriented; evidence-based; proportionate.

## Strategic themes and objectives
| Theme | Objective | Illustrative outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Critical services | Reduce exposure of priority services and dependencies | Current risk profiles and treatment plans |
| Identity | Strengthen identity lifecycle and privileged access | High-risk access governed and recertified |
| Secure delivery | Integrate security into architecture, change, cloud, and product delivery | Material initiatives engaged early |
| Detection/response | Improve visibility, triage, investigation, and coordination | Priority scenarios detectable and response-ready |
| Resilience | Align cyber recovery to service impact tolerances | Critical-service recovery exercises |
| Third parties | Prioritize suppliers by criticality, access, and concentration | Critical suppliers under risk-informed oversight |
| Leadership | Improve executive decisions with clear risk reporting | Exposure, ownership, options, and decisions visible |

## Target state
Strategy linked to business objectives; clear security accountabilities; risk ownership and escalation; prioritized capabilities; security integrated into major changes; tested response and recovery; transparent investment; executive measures that support decisions.

## Success criteria
Reduced material exposure; improved critical-service resilience; timely risk decisions; earlier security integration; evidence of investment outcomes; sustainable ownership and capability.

## Governance and refresh
CISO owns strategy development and execution; executive sponsor and delegated governance approve; ERM challenges and aggregates; board oversees material risk and strategic direction. Refresh for material business changes, acquisitions, service/technology shifts, incidents, regulatory change, appetite changes, funding changes, or performance concerns.
