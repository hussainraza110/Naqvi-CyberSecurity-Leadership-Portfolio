# Strategic Performance and Measurement Framework

## Principles
Measures support decisions, tie to objectives, define numerator/denominator/source/owner/frequency/limitations, distinguish activity from risk and outcomes, establish baselines, and avoid incentives to conceal weaknesses.

## Illustrative register
| ID | Measure | Type | Definition | Illustrative target/trigger | Owner | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Critical services with current cyber profile | KPI | Current approved profiles / all critical services | ≥95% | Cyber risk / service owners | Quarterly |
| M2 | Privileged accounts under approved control | KPI | Controlled in-scope accounts / total in-scope | ≥95% | IAM owner | Monthly |
| M3 | Critical vulnerabilities overdue | KRI | Count affecting critical services past approved date | Escalate material exposure | Technology owners | Monthly |
| M4 | Critical-service recovery exercises completed | KPI | Completed / annual approved plan | ≥90% | Resilience owner | Quarterly |
| M5 | Critical supplier reviews current | KPI | Current reviews / critical suppliers | ≥95% | Third-party risk | Quarterly |
| M6 | Material issues with named risk owner | KPI | Owned issues / material issues | 100% | Business/technology | Monthly |
| M7 | Expired exceptions without renewal | KRI | Count of expired exceptions | 0; escalate any | Security governance | Monthly |
| M8 | Initiatives engaged by design stage | KPI | Early-engaged in-scope initiatives / total | ≥90% | Tech portfolio / security architecture | Quarterly |
| M9 | Overdue priority exercise actions | KRI | Count of overdue high-priority actions | Escalate based on impact/age | IR owner | Monthly |
| M10 | Strategy milestones achieved on time | KPI | Achieved / due | ≥85% illustrative | CISO portfolio office | Monthly |

Targets are illustrative and must be calibrated to baseline, appetite, criticality, and data quality.

## Metric specification
Name/purpose; objective; type; calculation; scope/exclusions; data source/quality; baseline/date; target/threshold rationale; owner/steward; frequency; limitations; action on breach.

## Executive view
Material risk trend; appetite/exceptions; critical-service resilience; roadmap confidence/dependencies; investment and benefits; decisions and owners. Green status is not meaningful where coverage or data quality is weak.
