# Template — Strategy Development Canvas

## Business context
Organization/unit; business objectives; critical services; stakeholders; jurisdictions/obligations to validate; assumptions.

## Risk context
Material scenarios; business impacts; risk owners; appetite/tolerance; existing controls; evidence confidence.

## Strategy
Vision; mission; principles; themes; objectives/outcomes; target state.

## Execution
Initiatives; accountable owners; dependencies; funding/capacity; milestones; measures/baselines/targets.

## Governance
Executive sponsor; approval authority; risk acceptance path; review cadence; refresh triggers.

## Decisions
Decision; options; recommendation; consequences/residual risk; owner; date.
