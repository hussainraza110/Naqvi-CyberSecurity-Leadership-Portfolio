# Template — Annual Strategy Review Checklist

- [ ] Business strategy and operating model reviewed
- [ ] Critical services and dependencies validated
- [ ] Appetite and material scenarios reviewed
- [ ] Legal/regulatory obligations validated by owners
- [ ] Threat, incident, and exercise lessons considered
- [ ] Technology, cloud, sourcing, acquisition changes assessed
- [ ] Strategic objectives and target maturity remain relevant
- [ ] Operating model, workforce, and sourcing reviewed
- [ ] Roadmap, dependencies, budget, and capacity updated
- [ ] Benefits and risk outcomes assessed
- [ ] Deferred initiatives and residual risks documented
- [ ] KPI/KRI definitions and data quality reviewed
- [ ] Exceptions and acceptance records reviewed
- [ ] Executive/board feedback considered
- [ ] Refresh triggers and decisions documented
