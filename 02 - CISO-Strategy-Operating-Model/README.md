# Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy & Operating Model

**Portfolio project:** Folder 02 · **Case:** Meridian Financial Group (fictional) · **Audience:** Board, executive leadership, CISO, ERM, technology and security leaders.

## Purpose
A practical CISO playbook for establishing, transforming, and strategically refreshing cybersecurity in a large regulated financial-services enterprise. It connects business objectives, enterprise risk, governance, operating model, capabilities, investment, execution, and outcomes.

## Package
- `01-Strategic-Context/` — enterprise profile and assumptions
- `02-CISO-Strategy-Blueprint/` — vision, mission, objectives, target state
- `03-Cybersecurity-Operating-Model/` — organization, service delivery, sourcing, decision rights
- `04-Governance-Executive-Oversight/` — forums, escalation, risk acceptance
- `05-Capability-Maturity-Model/` — domains, baseline, targets, method
- `06-Strategy-Execution-Roadmap/` — phases, initiatives, dependencies
- `07-Investment-Budget-Strategy/` — illustrative funding and business-case approach
- `08-Performance-Measurement/` — KPI/KRI framework
- `09-CISO-Lifecycle-Playbook/` — greenfield, transformation, refresh
- `10-Executive-Decision-Scenarios/` — leadership dilemmas
- `templates/` — reusable working canvases
- `diagrams/` — Mermaid source diagrams
- `executive-briefing/` — board briefing and speaker prompts
- `references/` — framework reference notes

## Case-study conventions
Meridian Financial Group is entirely fictional. All company details, maturity scores, budgets, headcount, risk ratings, timelines, and targets are illustrative assumptions—not industry benchmarks or regulatory requirements. Validate actual obligations with legal, compliance, risk, business, and technology owners.

## Use
Read context and assumptions first; validate baseline; agree strategy and operating model; prioritize the roadmap and funding; assign owners; measure outcomes; refresh when business, risk, regulatory, or technology conditions materially change.
