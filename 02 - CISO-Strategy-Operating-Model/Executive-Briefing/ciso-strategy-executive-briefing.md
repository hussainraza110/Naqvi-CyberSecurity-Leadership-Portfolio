# CISO Strategy Executive Briefing — Meridian Financial Group

All details and figures are illustrative.

## Slide 1 — Strategic context
**Message:** Cybersecurity enables trusted digital growth and protects critical financial services. Meridian is expanding digital channels while managing hybrid technology, third-party dependency, and inconsistent security practices.

**Likely board question:** Why is this strategic?  
**Talking point:** Cyber events can disrupt services, trust, financial performance, and regulatory confidence; risk, resilience, investment, and accountability are enterprise decisions.

## Slide 2 — Strategic objectives
Protect critical services; strengthen identity; embed secure delivery; improve detection/response; build cyber resilience; govern third-party exposure; improve executive risk decisions.

**Likely question:** How do we know it works?  
**Talking point:** Validate risk reduction, resilience, accountable decisions, and outcomes—not tool counts.

## Slide 3 — Target operating model
Federated model. Central CISO provides strategy, standards, specialist capabilities, risk coordination, and oversight. Business/technology owners remain accountable for services, controls, and risk treatment.

**Likely question:** Does CISO own all cyber risk?  
**Talking point:** No. CISO advises, sets expectations, monitors, and escalates; authorized business and technology owners retain risk accountability.

## Slide 4 — Roadmap
0–6 months: governance, service profiles, baseline, priority identity/resilience. 7–18 months: standardize identity, secure delivery, detection, supplier oversight, measurement. 19–36 months: optimize, extend resilience testing, refresh workforce and strategy.

**Likely question:** What if delivery slips?  
**Talking point:** Re-sequence based on service risk, dependencies, capacity, and residual risk; escalate decisions.

## Slide 5 — Investment
Illustrative three-year transformation envelope PKR 1.8bn, separate from PKR 2.4bn annual run budget. Allocation supports identity, detection, secure delivery, resilience, third parties, governance data, workforce, and contingency.

**Likely question:** Does funding guarantee prevention?  
**Talking point:** No; it targets risk reduction and resilience. Track benefits and uncertainty; residual risk remains.

## Slide 6 — Decisions requested
Endorse strategic outcomes and federated model; confirm owners and acceptance pathways; sponsor service risk profiling; direct Finance/Technology/Risk/business owners to validate assumptions; require quarterly reporting.

**Likely question:** What happens next?  
**Talking point:** Validate baseline, finalize owners and initiatives, then return through delegated governance with validated funding and delivery plan.
