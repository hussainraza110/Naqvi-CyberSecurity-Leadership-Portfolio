# Strategy-to-Execution Roadmap

All phases and initiative sequencing are illustrative and require validation against risk, dependencies, funding, and capacity.

## Horizon 1 — Establish and stabilize (Months 0–6)
- Validate critical services, owners, and risk scenarios.
- Establish strategy governance, decision rights, and baseline reporting.
- Prioritize identity/PAM gaps and architecture patterns.
- Tier critical suppliers.
- Validate incident command and critical-service recovery plans.

## Horizon 2 — Standardize and integrate (Months 7–18)
- Expand identity lifecycle and privileged access governance.
- Integrate security into major technology delivery.
- Improve cloud/application security patterns and detection.
- Execute critical supplier treatment.
- Exercise cyber recovery for selected critical services.
- Establish outcome-based KPI/KRI reporting.

## Horizon 3 — Optimize and adapt (Months 19–36)
- Optimize based on evidence and risk movement.
- Extend service-based resilience and dependency testing.
- Automate where measurable risk/operating benefit exists.
- Refresh strategy, sourcing, and workforce.
- Incorporate lessons from incidents, exercises, and business changes.
