# Assumptions, Scope, and Boundaries

## Assumptions register
| ID | Illustrative assumption | Real-world validation |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Large regulated financial-services group | Business model, entities, jurisdictions |
| A2 | 12 critical services | BIA and service owners |
| A3 | Established but inconsistent security function | Evidence-based assessment |
| A4 | 95 security FTE | Role, workload, and sourcing inventory |
| A5 | PKR 2.4bn annual run budget | Finance-approved baseline |
| A6 | Average maturity near 2.4/5 | Scoring criteria and evidence |
| A7 | Three-year transformation | Planning cycle, dependencies, capacity |

## Included
Strategy, operating model, governance, capability maturity, execution roadmap, investment strategy, performance measurement, and lifecycle application.

## Excluded
Detailed control implementation, full obligation register, penetration-testing methodology, individual business continuity plans, vendor selection, and legal interpretation. These can inform the strategy but belong in specialist work products.

## Principles
1. Prioritize critical service impact over raw asset counts.
2. Prioritize material risk reduction and resilience over tool accumulation.
3. Business and technology owners remain accountable for their risks.
4. Risk acceptance is made by an authorized owner within delegated authority.
5. Exceptions are documented, time-bound, and reviewed.
6. Strategy is evidence-led and refreshed when assumptions materially change.
