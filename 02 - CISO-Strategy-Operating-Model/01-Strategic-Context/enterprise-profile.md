# Meridian Financial Group — Fictional Enterprise Profile

All figures and details are illustrative.

Meridian Financial Group (MFG) is a diversified financial-services group comprising retail/commercial banking, digital payments, and investment/wealth services. It operates across three countries with centralized corporate functions and business-unit technology teams. Its estate includes legacy platforms, data centers, cloud, SaaS, and third-party services.

| Cybersecurity team | 95 FTE |

| Transformation planning horizon | 3 years |

## Business strategy themes
- Grow trusted digital channels and customer adoption.
- Improve efficiency and simplify technology.
- Expand payment and wealth capabilities through partners.
- Maintain trust, availability, and regulatory confidence.
- Improve data-informed decisions while protecting sensitive information.

## Critical services
Customer account access and transaction processing; payments; digital banking; merchant processing; wealth transactions; customer identity/authentication; financial and regulatory reporting. Service impact tolerances, dependencies, and recovery objectives require validation by service owners.

## Current-state challenges
Security capabilities exist but are uneven across units. Key challenges include unclear risk ownership, fragmented identity and privileged access, variable cloud patterns, late security engagement in change, inconsistent executive reporting, and supplier assurance not always prioritized by criticality.

## Stakeholders
Board/Board Risk Committee; CEO; CFO; COO; CIO/CTO; CRO; business-unit leaders; General Counsel/Compliance; Internal Audit; CISO leadership.

## Strategic constraints
Competing investments, specialist talent scarcity, legacy dependencies, business-unit autonomy, third-party concentration, and the need to transform without disrupting critical services.

**Strategic implication:** Begin with business outcomes, material scenarios, decision rights, and service resilience—not a tool shopping list.
