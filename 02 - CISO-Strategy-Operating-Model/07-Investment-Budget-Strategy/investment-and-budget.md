# Cybersecurity Investment and Budget Strategy

All financial values are fictional planning assumptions in PKR.

## Illustrative baseline
Annual security run budget: PKR 2.4bn. Proposed three-year transformation envelope: PKR 1.8bn (planning average PKR 600m/year), separate from run budget. Finance must validate cost classification and total cost of ownership.

Not a benchmark or prescribed mix. Recalibrate using risk, current capability, architecture, costs, and business priorities.

## Decision criteria
Material risk reduction; critical service impact; obligation; resilience/business enablement; dependency; feasibility/capacity; lifecycle cost; benefits evidence; provider concentration and exit.

## Governance
CISO develops portfolio and rationale; business/technology validate outcomes and dependencies; Finance challenges cost and affordability; CRO/Compliance advise; delegated budget authority approves; benefits owners report outcomes.

## Business case
Decision requested; business context/risk scenario; options including defer/do nothing; expected risk reduction/residual risk; cost/resources; dependencies and delivery risks; benefits/measurements; recommendation and accountable owner. Avoid false precision in loss avoidance; state uncertainty and ranges.
