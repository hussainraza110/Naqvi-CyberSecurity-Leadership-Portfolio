# Governance and Executive Oversight

## Governance objectives
Align cyber decisions with appetite; establish ownership and authority; escalate material exposure; monitor strategy and investment; provide board oversight without displacing management accountability.

## Illustrative forums
| Forum | Sponsor / chair | Purpose | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board Risk Committee | Board | Oversight of material risk, resilience, strategy | Quarterly |
| Executive Risk Committee | CRO / CEO delegate | Cross-enterprise risk and appetite decisions | Quarterly / as needed |
| Cyber Risk Steering Committee | CISO / sponsor | Strategy, risk treatment, prioritization | Monthly |
| Security Design Authority | CIO/CTO delegate | Design decisions and exceptions | Monthly / as needed |
| CISO Leadership Team | CISO | Delivery, capacity, metrics, readiness | Weekly |
| Critical Service Resilience Forum | COO / service owner | Recovery gaps and exercises | Quarterly |

Use existing forums when they have appropriate authority.

## Decision rights
| Decision | Recommender | Accountable owner |
|---|---|---|
| Cyber strategy | CISO | Executive sponsor / delegated body |
| Security policy | CISO | Designated policy authority |
| Business risk acceptance | CISO advises | Authorized business/risk owner |
| Architecture exception | Security architecture | Designated authority or risk owner |
| Investment | CISO + Finance partner | Budget authority |
| Incident business decisions | Incident command / CISO advises | Designated executive incident authority |
| Recovery priority | Service owner / resilience lead | Business service owner |

## Exception record
Affected service; scenario and impact; controls; residual risk; named owner; approval authority; rationale; expiry; review cadence; treatment milestones.

## Escalate when
Risk exceeds appetite/authority; critical service impact tolerance may be breached; material incident is suspected; treatment is unfunded/refused; exception expires; critical supplier exposure is unmanaged; roadmap blockage changes expected risk outcomes.

## Board reporting
Answer: What changed? Which risks are outside/near appetite? Business impact? Is management treatment effective? What decisions are needed? What uncertainty remains? Include trends, owners, treatment, resilience, and decision requests—not just technical activity counts.
