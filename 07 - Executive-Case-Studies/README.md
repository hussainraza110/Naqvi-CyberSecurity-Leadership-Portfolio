# Executive Cybersecurity Case Studies

**Organization:** Meridian Financial Group (fictional regulated financial services enterprise). All case facts, financial values, and outcomes are illustrative.

## Cases
1. Ransomware resilience and recovery investment
2. Cloud transformation risk trade-off
3. Third-party concentration and continuity
4. Identity compromise and privileged access
5. Budget-constrained investment prioritization
6. Board decision brief and lessons
7. Executive case-study template
8. Scenario assumptions and use notes

Cases support executive decision-making, workshops, and portfolio discussion; they are not actual incident reports or legal advice.
