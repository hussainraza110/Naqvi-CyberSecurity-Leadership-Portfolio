# Scenario Assumptions and Use Notes

These are educational scenarios for a fictional regulated financial services enterprise—not actual incident reports, legal opinions, or descriptions of any real organization.

- Distinguish scenario facts from assumptions and recommendations.
- Do not claim breach, exfiltration, control effectiveness, or financial impact unless stated.
- Treat metrics and costs as illustrative absent validated sources.
- Confirm jurisdiction-specific obligations with legal/compliance owners.
- Obtain technical, legal, finance, and business review before operational use.
- Sanitize real organizational details before adapting these cases.
