# Case 05 — Budget-Constrained Security Investment

The annual budget cannot fund every proposal. Competing needs include identity, detection, resilience, data protection, and supplier risk.

## Decision method
1. Identify mandatory obligations and deadlines.
2. Rank proposals by severity, control gap, business criticality, urgency, confidence, readiness, and dependencies.
3. Compare options and TCO.
4. Define minimum viable treatment and consequences of deferral.
5. Stage funding against delivery and outcome gates.

## Illustrative choice
Prioritize critical identity and recovery gaps; then detection and exposure visibility based on dependencies and capacity. Maintain a deferred list with residual risk, owner, and review trigger.

Budget constraints do not eliminate risk: they force explicit decisions on treatment, deferral, and acceptance. Measure risk evidence, mandatory action timeliness, forecast accuracy, and benefit realization.
