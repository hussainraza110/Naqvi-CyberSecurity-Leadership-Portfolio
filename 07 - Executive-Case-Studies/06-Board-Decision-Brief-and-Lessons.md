# Case 06 — Board Decision Brief

## Decision requested
Approve a staged, risk-informed cyber resilience and identity investment program, with quarterly reporting on outcomes, forecast, delivery confidence, and residual risk.

## Options
- Fund minimum mandatory remediation.
- Fund a balanced staged portfolio.
- Defer and accept documented residual risk.

## Recommendation
Fund the balanced portfolio, initially focused on critical services and high-impact scenarios. Release later tranches only after evidence and gates.

## Conditions
Named risk/business owners; Finance-validated estimates; clear scope and outcomes; formal exception handling; quarterly review and event-driven escalation.

## Lessons
Business-service outcomes matter; recovery requires tests; scores do not replace accountability; delivery progress differs from risk reduction; transparent deferral beats unrecorded underfunding.
