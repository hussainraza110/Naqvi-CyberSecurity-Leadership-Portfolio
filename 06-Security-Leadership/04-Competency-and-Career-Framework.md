# Competency and Career Framework

## Competencies
Technical depth; risk and business context; executive communication; delivery judgment; collaboration and coaching; ethics and accountability.

| Level | Observable behavior |
|---|---|
| 1 Foundation | Understands concepts; works with guidance |
| 2 Practitioner | Independently delivers defined work |
| 3 Senior | Handles ambiguity, mentors, improves practice |
| 4 Lead | Sets cross-team direction and influences stakeholders |
| 5 Executive | Aligns capability and investment to enterprise outcomes |

Career paths include specialist, engineering, operations, risk/GRC, architecture, product security, and people leadership. Reward impact and capability; do not require experts to become managers.

Development plan: target capability, evidence gap, measurable objective, learning, stretch assignment, mentor, completion evidence, review date. Calibrate fairly; certifications and tenure alone are insufficient.
