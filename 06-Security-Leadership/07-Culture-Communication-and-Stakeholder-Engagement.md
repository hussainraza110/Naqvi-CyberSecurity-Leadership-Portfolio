# Culture, Communication, and Stakeholder Engagement

## Behaviors
Explain business risk and outcomes; invite challenge; make trade-offs explicit; protect psychological safety; recognize prevention and learning, not only crisis heroics.

| Stakeholder | Need | Engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Board / Risk Committee | Material risk and decisions | Quarterly reporting |
| CFO | Cost and value evidence | Monthly portfolio review |
| CIO / Technology | Feasible controls and sequencing | Architecture forums |
| Business leaders | Service impact and risk choices | Business risk reviews |
| HR | Capability and wellbeing | Quarterly talent planning |
| Procurement / Legal | Supplier and contractual risk | Lifecycle checkpoints |

Communication sequence: context; risk/decision; options; recommendation; owner/date; feedback channel. Use retrospectives and surveys, then publish what changed.
