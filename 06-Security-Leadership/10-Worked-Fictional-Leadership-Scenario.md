# Worked Fictional Leadership Scenario

## Situation
Digital-channel growth increases alert volume and product release pace. Specialist roles become single points of failure; employee fatigue rises. Executives ask for faster delivery.

## Diagnosis
This is not automatically a headcount problem: demand is poorly segmented, repetitive work is not automated, service ownership is unclear, and skill coverage is uneven.

## Response
1. Segment workload by risk and service criticality.
2. Tune low-value alert sources and automate enrichment.
3. Embed security partners with priority product teams.
4. Cross-train responders and test runbooks.
5. Compare hire, develop, automate, and managed-service scenarios.
6. Track service outcomes and workload together.

## Decision and success criteria
Approve targeted capability investment and workflow simplification in stages. Validate demand before committing to headcount. Success means less untriaged high-risk work, better secure-delivery lead time, tested deputy coverage, and sustainable on-call load. Baseline results before setting numeric targets.
