# Succession, Resilience, and Talent Risk

Critical roles create material risk when absence affects response, regulatory accountability, control operation, specialist knowledge, or continuity.

| Risk | Indicator | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Single point of failure | One qualified owner | Deputy, cross-training, runbook |
| Scarce-skill attrition | Vacancy duration / regretted attrition | Development and market review |
| On-call fatigue | Excess pages or overtime | Rotation and automation |
| Knowledge concentration | Untested handover | Pairing, documentation, exercises |
| Supplier dependency | Provider-only capability | Exit plan and knowledge transfer |

Succession plan records interim cover, potential successor, readiness horizon, development, delegation/access, and review date. Monitor workload and leave; include talent continuity in resilience plans.
