# Cyber Crisis Communications Pack

Templates are illustrative. Validate facts, content, and timing with Legal, Privacy, Regulatory, and Communications teams.

## Internal holding message
**Subject:** Service disruption — verified updates to follow

We are responding to a technology security incident affecting selected services. Response teams are containing the issue and assessing impact. Follow manager instructions and approved channels. Do not speculate, share screenshots, or communicate externally. Next update: [time/channel].

## Customer holding statement
We are investigating a technology security incident affecting [service/region]. Our teams are working to protect customers and restore service safely. [Insert verified facts only; state if scope is under investigation.] Updates will be posted through [official channel] at [time]. For assistance, use [verified support channel].

## Regulator notification preparation
Organization/contact; discovery time/status; affected services/systems/data/customer groups (known/unknown); actual/potential impact; containment/recovery; third-party involvement; confidentiality/integrity/availability assessment; timeline; next update; open questions/investigation. This is not a statement of legal deadlines.

## Media holding line
We are responding to a cybersecurity incident affecting [verified scope]. Our priority is protecting customers and restoring operations safely. The investigation is ongoing; we will not speculate about unverified claims. Confirmed information will be shared through [official channel].

## Executive briefing
Confirmed facts; unknowns; service/customer impact; containment; decisions required; next update owner/time.

## Approval flow
Communications drafts using Incident Commander facts; Legal/Privacy and business owner review; delegated executive approves; release via authorized channel; log version, approver, timestamp, audience, and next update.
