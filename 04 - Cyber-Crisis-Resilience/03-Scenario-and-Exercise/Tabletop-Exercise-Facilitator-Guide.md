# Tabletop Exercise — Facilitator Guide

**Duration:** 2.5–3 hours (illustrative)  
**Participants:** Executive Crisis Lead, Crisis Manager, Incident Commander, CISO, CIO, COO/service owner, CRO, Legal, Communications, CFO, supplier lead, observers.  
**Mode:** Discussion only; no production actions.

## Ground rules
Respond in role, state assumptions/confidence, capture decision/owner/rationale/time/evidence, and assess the plan rather than individuals.

## Injects
| Time | Inject | Discussion / expected action |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 | Suspicious privileged activity; payments degraded | Severity, incident command, crisis trigger, evidence |
| 00:20 | Ransomware indicators; scope uncertain | Containment options, identity trust, service impact |
| 00:40 | Actor claims data exfiltration, unverified | Legal/privacy, evidence, uncertainty, holding message |
| 01:00 | Backup admin may share compromised identity | Isolate recovery plane, clean trust, backup integrity |
| 01:20 | Payments disruption threshold exceeded; supplier down | Continuity, service priorities, supplier escalation |
| 01:40 | Regulator/customer inquiries and media questions | Approval path, factual lines, update timing |
| 02:00 | Restore candidate available; integrity checks incomplete | Restore gates, business sign-off, residual-risk authority |
| 02:20 | Core recovery works; backlog and uncertainty remain | Stand-down, customer response, review, board update |

## Facilitator prompts
Who activates crisis governance? Who can isolate shared identity? How are facts separated from assumptions? What must be true before restoration? Who accepts residual risk? What is the next stakeholder update? What evidence proves objectives were met?

## Evaluation (0–3)
| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activation/command | Absent | Late/unclear | Activated with gaps | Timely and clear |
| Situation awareness | Conflicting | Fragmented | Shared, some gaps | Clear facts/confidence/impact |
| Decisions | Unowned | Ad hoc | Logged with gaps | Timely and traceable |
| Containment/evidence | Unsafe | Partial | Coordinated with gaps | Proportionate/evidence-aware |
| Recovery | No gates | Unclear | Gates with gaps | Clean and business-validated |
| Communications | Unsupported | Delayed | Approved, uneven | Timely and consistent |
| Learning | None | Generic | Owners/dates | Prioritized and validated |

## Hotwash
What worked, failed, surprised, or invalidated assumptions? Create prioritized actions with accountable owner, due date, dependency, closure evidence, and validation.
