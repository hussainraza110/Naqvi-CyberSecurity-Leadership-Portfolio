# Case 04 — Identity Compromise

## Scenario
Investigation finds suspicious use of a privileged account. The scenario does not assume confirmed exfiltration.

## Immediate response
- Activate incident command and preserve logs/evidence.
- Revoke or constrain suspected credentials through emergency procedures.
- Assess privileged sessions and lateral movement.
- Coordinate disruptive containment with service owners.
- Communicate confirmed facts separately from hypotheses.

## Strategic decision
Fund PAM, identity threat detection, and lifecycle controls through a risk-tiered rollout. Balance operational friction from tighter controls against compromise blast radius. Emergency access must be controlled, monitored, tested, and reviewed.

Measures: privileged coverage, standing privilege reduction, session visibility, emergency access test success, and containment time.
