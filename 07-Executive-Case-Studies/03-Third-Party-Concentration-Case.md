# Case 03 — Third-Party Concentration

A critical payment service relies on a limited set of providers. Point-in-time assurance is strong, yet correlated outage could affect multiple services.

**Decision:** accept with monitoring, diversify, redesign, or combine tested continuity with staged diversification?

**Recommendation:** risk-based diversification and tested exit/continuity plans. Rank dependencies by business impact, substitutability, recovery time, and correlated exposure.

Executive actions: map fourth parties/shared infrastructure; confirm SLA, notification, data access, and exit terms; test failover and portability; assign risk owner; review concentration quarterly.

Measures: critical suppliers with tested plans, service concentration, overdue remediation, and demonstrated failover performance.
