# Framework Alignment & Scenario Assumptions

## Alignment notes
Conceptual alignment may include:
- **NIST CSF 2.0:** Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- **NIST SP 800-61:** incident handling lifecycle concepts; verify against current publication.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** ISMS, risk treatment, performance evaluation, improvement.
- **ISO 22301:** business continuity, impact analysis, exercises, recovery.
- **ISO 22320:** incident management and coordination.
- **COBIT / Three Lines:** governance, accountability, risk oversight, independent assurance.

Illustrative mapping only—not certification or compliance assertion. Verify current sources, laws, regulations, contracts, and internal policy.

## Assumptions and cautions
Meridian is fictional; it operates banking, digital payments, and investment services, with hybrid technology and critical third parties. The ransomware case includes possible privileged identity compromise, disruption, and unverified exfiltration. Roles, metrics, timelines, thresholds, and objectives are invented. This package is not legal advice, does not prescribe ransom payment, and states no notification deadlines.

Adapt authority/on-call model, confirm notification obligations with counsel, align thresholds to service impact and appetite, technically validate backup/identity/recovery architecture, and exercise with internal teams and suppliers.
