# Ransomware Crisis Scenario — Meridian Financial Group

> Fictional exercise scenario; no real organization or incident is represented.

## Situation
A ransomware operator compromises a privileged identity pathway and disrupts shared technology dependencies. A critical payments service is degraded. Some endpoints show encryption indicators; an actor claims sensitive data was copied, but exfiltration is unverified.

## Initial facts (T+0)
- SOC detects suspicious privileged authentication and abnormal administrative activity.
- Payments operations reports processing delays and unavailable workstations.
- Endpoint telemetry shows ransomware-like behavior on a limited server segment.
- Backup administration may share identity dependencies with production.
- Scope, impact, persistence, and data theft remain uncertain.

## Objectives
1. Activate appropriate incident/crisis governance.
2. Establish a shared operating picture with confidence labels.
3. Make proportionate containment decisions while protecting critical services.
4. Coordinate legal, privacy, regulatory, and communications assessment.
5. Prioritize clean recovery and business validation.
6. Record decisions, trade-offs, and actions.

## Complications
Privileged identity trust may be compromised; a supplier supports a payments dependency; recovery evidence is incomplete; business leaders press for restoration before technical confidence; stakeholders ask questions before forensic conclusions.
