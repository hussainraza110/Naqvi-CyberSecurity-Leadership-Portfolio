# 04 — Cyber Crisis & Resilience

Portfolio package for fictional **Meridian Financial Group**, a large regulated financial-services enterprise (banking, digital payments, investment services).

> All organization details, scenarios, roles, metrics, objectives, and values are fictional and illustrative. No confidential employer information is included.

## Contents
- `01-Crisis-Governance/` — crisis playbook and command structure
- `02-Incident-Response/` — incident response plan
- `03-Scenario-and-Exercise/` — ransomware scenario and tabletop facilitator guide
- `04-Communications/` — stakeholder message templates
- `05-Resilience-and-Recovery/` — service resilience and cyber recovery
- `06-Metrics-and-Improvement/` — KPI/KRI and post-incident review
- `07-Templates/` — SITREP, decision log, recovery validation, review
- `08-Diagrams/` — Mermaid workflows
- `09-Reference/` — assumptions and alignment notes

Distinguish incident response (technical handling), crisis management (executive coordination and decisions), business continuity (sustain priority activities), and cyber recovery (restore trusted technology and service).
